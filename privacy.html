Privacy policy

Effective 26 November 2026 · Applies to the Warq app for Android and desktop

Warq is a reader for the EPUB and PDF books you already own. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not collect, sell or share any personal data.

Your books and reading data stay on your device

Books you add, your reading positions, highlights, notes, bookmarks, collections, reading goals and settings are stored only inside the app on your device. Warq has no servers and never uploads any of it.

If you use Back up library, Warq creates a backup file and hands it to your device's share or save options. Where that file goes (for example a cloud drive or another app) is your choice, and is covered by that service's own privacy policy.

What uses the internet

  • Natural voice download. If you choose to download the natural read-aloud voice, Warq downloads a voice file (about 42 MB) from GitHub, where this website is hosted. Like any web request, GitHub receives your device's IP address; see GitHub's privacy statement. Nothing about you or your books is sent. After this one download, read-aloud works offline.
  • Define. When you choose Define on a word, Warq asks the Free Dictionary API (dictionaryapi.dev) for its meaning and shows it in the app. Only that word is sent.
  • Translate and web search. When you choose Translate, or search the web for a word, Warq opens Google Translate or Google Search in your browser with the text you selected. Only that text is sent, and only to the site you open; that site's own privacy policy applies.

Nothing else in Warq connects to the internet.

Permissions

  • Internet: only for the uses above.
  • Foreground service (media playback), wake lock and notifications: so reading aloud keeps playing with the screen off and shows playback controls on your lock screen and in notifications.
  • Files you pick: Warq reads only the files you choose to import or restore.

Children

Warq does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Removing a book deletes it and its notes from the app. Uninstalling Warq, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything it stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
